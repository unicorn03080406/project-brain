Exceptions report requirements (from Priya, 2026-09-12)

The report goes out daily at 07:00 local time to ops-leads@northwind.example.
It lists every shipment that entered EXCEPTION status in the previous 24 hours, grouped by
reason (damaged, lost, refused, address issue). Each line shows the shipment id, carrier
reference, last known location and the time the exception was raised.
If there were no exceptions, the email still goes out and says so.
The report must not include customer names or addresses.
